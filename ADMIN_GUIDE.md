## 2. Managing Team Registrations

- Click on the **Registrations** tab.
- You will see every registered team, their college, branch, team size, and current status.
- Use the **Search Box** to search by Team ID (`IDN-XXXX`), student name, USN, or college.
- Click **View Details** on any team to inspect full member rosters, dietary preferences (Veg/Non-Veg), t-shirt sizes, and problem statements.

---

## 3. Verifying UPI Fee Payments (₹100)

Every team pays ₹100 via the official UPI QR during registration.

1. Click on the **Payments** tab.
2. Teams waiting for verification have an amber `Pending` badge.
3. Compare the **Transaction Reference (UTR)** and uploaded screenshot against your Google Pay / PhonePe / bank statement for Anveshan Club.
4. Click **Mark Verified**:
   - The team status immediately updates to `Verified`.
   - An automated email is dispatched to the team leader confirming their pass.
5. If the reference is invalid or money did not credit, click **Reject Payment**.

## 4. Event Day Desk Check-In & QR Pass Printing (18 November 2026)

On the morning of the event (8:00 AM – 9:30 AM IST):

1. **Option A — From the Login Screen**:
   - Go to `/?view=admin`.
   - Click the **Scan QR to Verify** toggle at the top of the authentication card.
   - Click **Start Camera Scanner** (or drag/upload a pass screenshot).
2. **Option B — Inside the Admin Console**:
   - Log in and open the **Check-In Desk** tab.
3. **Verify Attendees in Real-Time**:
   - Point the camera at the attendee's digital pass QR (on their phone screen or paper printout).
   - An audio chime confirms verification instantly.
   - The system checks payment, marks the team **CHECKED-IN**, and displays their full team roster.
4. **Print Official Physical Entry Pass**:
   - Click the prominent **Print Official Entry Pass** button (or press `Ctrl+P`).
   - The system formats a print-ready badge with BIET credentials, Team ID stamp, member USNs, T-shirt sizes, and meal allocations.
   - Hand over the physical pass badge, lunch coupons, and lab kit.

---

## 5. Publishing Live Announcements

1. Click on the **Announcements** tab.
2. Enter the **Title** (e.g., `WiFi Credentials & Lab Workbench Allotment`).
3. Select the **Category**:
   - `Update` (General information)
   - `Urgent` (Important alerts)
   - `Deadline` (Submission reminders)
   - `Result` (Shortlist & prize ceremony notices)
4. Check **Pin to top** if you want this announcement highlighted at the very top of the public website.
5. Click **Publish Post Live**. It immediately appears on the website for all participants.

---

## 6. Updating the Core Team & Contact Helplines

1. Click on the **Core Team** tab.
2. Use **Add Coordinator** to add new faculty advisors or student volunteers.
3. Toggle phone numbers to **Public** if their contact should be listed for student queries, or hide them for faculty.

---

## 7. Exporting Attendee Data for Certificates & Badges

1. Go to the **Overview** or **Registrations** tab.
2. Click **Export All Teams (CSV / Excel)**.
3. An Excel-compatible `.csv` file will download instantly with all team details, member names, USNs, emails, phone numbers, and check-in times.
